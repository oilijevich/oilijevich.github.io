About

Omega Ilijevich is a freelance journalist based in Chicago, Illinois. Her beats include local politics, lifestyle and culture.

She graduated from the University of Virginia in 2023, and is currently pursuing a Master's in Journalism from Northwestern University's Medill School.

Her work has appeared in print in The Cavalier Daily and Washingtonian Magazine and over the air at WMRA, WTJU and VPM News.
